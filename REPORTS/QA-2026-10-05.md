## SEO / Brand Issues

### 2. clinic-chatbot.html — Unprotected client demo

`clinic-chatbot.html` is a standalone Northview Psychology demo embed:
- `<title>` is **"Northview Psychology — AI Receptionist"**
- **No `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`** — Google will crawl and index it
- **No meta description, no canonical, no og/twitter tags**
- Could appear in Google SERPs as "Northview Psychology — AI Receptionist" under trivox.ca

**Fix:** Add `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` to `<head>`.
