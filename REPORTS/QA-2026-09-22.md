## 4. Hidden-Content / Mobile Bugs

**Status: ✅ CLEAN**

### [data-reveal] system:
- **motion.css** sets `opacity:0; transform:translateY(26px)` on `[data-reveal]` elements.
- **motion.js** uses `IntersectionObserver` with `threshold: 0.15` and `rootMargin: '0px 0px -8% 0px'`. At 15% threshold, even tall sections on small phones will trigger well before the element would be below the fold edge. **No mobile stuck-invisible risk.**
- GSAP reveals (scroll.js, inline on index.html) animate **transform only** — the inline comment confirms this is intentional: "Opacity fade is handled by motion.js [data-reveal]. This guarantees no element can ever get stuck invisible if a ScrollTrigger misfires." Safe design.
- `ScrollTrigger.refresh()` is called at both `window.load` and `setTimeout 700ms` — mitigates layout-shift related stuck triggers.

No elements combining opacity:0 with a high intersection threshold risk.
