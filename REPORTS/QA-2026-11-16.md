## 4. Hidden-Content / Mobile Bugs — CLEAN ✅

- `[data-reveal]` pattern uses `IntersectionObserver` with `threshold: 0.15` and `rootMargin: '0px 0px -8% 0px'` — safe on mobile, no tall-element trap risk
