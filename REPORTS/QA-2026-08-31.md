## 🟡 MEDIUM — `clinic-chatbot.html` Missing noindex / SEO Guard

`clinic-chatbot.html` is a live client-demo chatbot widget (Northview Psychology AI receptionist). It has **no** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`, no canonical, no description, and no og: tags.

**Impact:** Google will index this page as:
- Title: "Northview Psychology — AI Receptionist"
- No description (Google will auto-generate one from the JS/markup)
- The page appears under **trivox.ca** as if TRIVOX is Northview Psychology.

This creates brand confusion in search results and could harm TRIVOX's SEO by surfacing a thin demo page.

**Fix:** Add `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` to clinic-chatbot.html, or add a proper TRIVOX description and canonical pointing to the clinic case study page.
