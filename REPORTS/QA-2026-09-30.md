## 4. Hidden-Content / Mobile Bugs

**✅ Clean (no threshold risk).**

- `[data-reveal]` — motion.css sets `opacity:0; transform:translateY(26px)`. Revealed by motion.js IntersectionObserver at **threshold: 0.15** with `rootMargin: '0px 0px -8% 0px'`. A 0.15 threshold is safe: only 15% of the element needs to be visible, so tall sections on mobile will reveal fine.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback immediately adds `.in` to all reveals — no stuck-hidden risk for accessibility users ✅
- GSAP scroll reveals (index.html) use `start: 'top 82%'` / `top 85%` — generous thresholds, safe on mobile ✅
- `section.vault.reveal` is animated by GSAP (not IntersectionObserver), triggered at `start: 'top 70%'` — safe ✅
- No `visibility:hidden`, `display:none` or `opacity:0` applied outside the reveal pattern ✅
