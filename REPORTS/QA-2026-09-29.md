## 3. Accessibility

**⚠️ ISSUE — Heading level skip: h2 → h4 on 3 pages (LOW)**

The following pages skip `<h3>` when transitioning from a section `<h2>` to card-level headings:
- `index.html` (line 324–326): "Why we exist" values cards use `<h4>` directly under an `<h2>`.
- `case-car-deals-direct.html` (lines 137–139): Problem/System/Result cards use `<h4>` inside an `<h2>` block.
- `case-clinic-receptionist.html` (lines 137–139): Same PSR pattern.

These are visually intentional card-label headings, but the jump from h2 to h4 violates WCAG SC 1.3.1 (screen readers announce a missing level). Fix: change `<h4>` to `<h3>` in these card patterns, or use a `<p>` with a bold style if they are not semantic section headings.
