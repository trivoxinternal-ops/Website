## LOW — SEO Meta Gaps

### clinic-chatbot.html
Missing: `<meta name="description">`, `<link rel="canonical">`, `og:*` tags, `twitter:*` tags.
This is an embedded demo widget (fictional clinic chatbot) — may be intentionally bare — but if the page is indexable it should have at minimum a canonical pointing elsewhere or `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`.

### privacy.html
Missing: `<meta name="description">`, `<link rel="canonical">`.
Privacy pages don't require og: tags, but a canonical and brief description are good practice and prevent duplicate-content penalties if the page is ever syndicated.
