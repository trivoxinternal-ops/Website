## 3. Accessibility

**`clinic-chatbot.html`:** ❌ No `<h1>` found (the page uses no semantic headings at all — it's a chat UI).

**Heading level skips (all marketing pages):** ⚠️ h2 → h4 (h3 is skipped) appears consistently across 9 pages:
- `index.html`, `ai-automation-gta.html`, `ai-automation-mississauga.html`, `ai-automation-toronto.html`, `ai-receptionist.html`, `ai-voice-agents.html`, `case-car-deals-direct.html`, `case-clinic-receptionist.html`, `workflow-automation.html`

This is likely an intentional visual style choice (h4 used for card labels), but screen readers will flag it. Consider switching those elements to `<p class="label">` or `<span>` instead of `<h4>` to avoid the skip.

All pages have `lang="en"`, `viewport` meta, and `<title>`. No missing `alt` attributes found.

## 4. Hidden-Content / Mobile Bugs

**motion.js `[data-reveal]`:** threshold=0.15, rootMargin=`0px 0px -8% 0px` — safe for mobile. Elements start at `opacity:0` and transition to visible on scroll.

**index.html counter animation (inline script):** Uses `threshold:.5` on IntersectionObserver for count-up numbers. If a counter element isn't 50% visible on a small phone screen it won't animate. **Not a content bug** (text still renders; it just won't count up) — but worth noting. Not flagged as critical.

**`[data-reveal]` across 9 pages:** Motion.js handles all reveals at threshold=0.15. The `scroll.js` (GSAP ScrollTrigger, transform-only) is architecturally clean per its own comment: "no opacity, so nothing can get stuck invisible." No high-risk mobile hidden-content issue found.
