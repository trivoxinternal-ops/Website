## 4. Hidden-Content / Mobile Bugs

**Reveal system:** `[data-reveal]` starts at `opacity:0; transform:translateY(26px)`. Triggered via IntersectionObserver in `motion.js` with **threshold: 0.15** and `rootMargin: 0px 0px -8% 0px`.

**Assessment: SAFE.** Threshold 0.15 is well below the 0.4 danger zone. Elements only need 15% visibility to trigger — even on a tall mobile element, this fires reliably.

Additional safeguards in place:
- GSAP ScrollTrigger (used on sub-pages) calls `ScrollTrigger.refresh()` on window load and after 700ms — guards against stuck-hidden sections from late-loading images/fonts ✅
- `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback forces all `[data-reveal]` to `.in` immediately ✅
- `.vault.reveal` on index.html uses `will-change` only (no opacity:0 on `.reveal` class itself) ✅

**No mobile hidden-content bugs found.**
