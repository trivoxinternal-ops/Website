## 4. Hidden-content / Mobile Bugs

**Clean — no mobile stuck-invisible risk.** ✅

- `[data-reveal]` elements use `opacity:0` + `translateY(26px)` via `motion.css`, revealed by IntersectionObserver in `motion.js` at **threshold: 0.15** with `rootMargin: '0px 0px -8% 0px'`. Threshold of 15% is safe on mobile — elements will trigger well before they'd be missed.
- GSAP ScrollTrigger animations on service pages use `start: 'top 82%'` or `'top 70%'` — both safe thresholds.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` handled: motion.css forces `opacity:1; transform:none` when set.
- The `.vault` section uses class `reveal` (not `[data-reveal]`) and is animated separately by GSAP — not affected by IntersectionObserver.
