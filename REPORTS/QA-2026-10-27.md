### 4. clinic-chatbot.html — no SEO meta

`clinic-chatbot.html` has no `<meta name="description">`, no Open Graph tags, and no `<link rel="canonical">`. It also has no `<h1>`. This is likely intentional (it's a full-screen demo app, not a content page), but if it's ever linked publicly it will appear unstyled in social previews with no description.

**Action if intentional:** Add `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` to prevent accidental indexing.
