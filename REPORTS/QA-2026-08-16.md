## 1. Missing Pages — HIGH (404 errors + sitemap damage)

Two pages are listed in `sitemap.xml` AND linked from multiple published pages, but the files **do not exist** in the repo. Every visitor or bot that follows these links hits a 404.

| Missing file | Where it's linked |
|---|---|
| `what-is-geo.html` | `index.html` (FAQ), `ai-automation-toronto.html` (ledger row), `ai-receptionist.html` (body text) |
| `get-recommended-by-chatgpt.html` | `index.html` (FAQ), `ai-automation-toronto.html` (ledger row), `ai-receptionist.html` (body text) |

**Impact:** Internal links broken for visitors + both URLs in `sitemap.xml` → Google will crawl them, hit 404, and may downgrade the entire site's crawl budget. These appear to be planned GEO/SEO content pages that were added to the sitemap before being published.

**Fix:** Either create these pages or remove the links and sitemap entries until the content is ready.

## 2. Stale Booking Links — MEDIUM (wrong booking URL on 2 pages)

`workflow-automation.html` and `case-car-deals-direct.html` still use the **old Calendly link** (`https://calendly.com/trivoxinternal/intro-call`). Every other page uses the current Cal.com link (`https://cal.com/trivox-interno-hfbm3x/trivox-discovery-call`).

| File | Instances |
|---|---|
| `workflow-automation.html` | 6 — nav "Book a call", hero CTA, 4× ledger rows, footer CTA, + schema JSON |
| `case-car-deals-direct.html` | 3 — nav "Book a call", hero CTA, footer CTA |

**Impact:** Leads who click "Book a call" on the Workflow Automation page or the Car Deals Direct case study land on the old/inactive Calendly. That's a broken conversion funnel on two of the more frequently-linked pages.

**Fix:** Global find-and-replace `calendly.com/trivoxinternal/intro-call` → `cal.com/trivox-interno-hfbm3x/trivox-discovery-call` in both files.

## 3. clinic-chatbot.html — LOW (demo page missing SEO meta, indexed in sitemap)

`clinic-chatbot.html` is a self-contained demo (Northview Psychology AI receptionist) with no SEO meta tags and is listed in `sitemap.xml`.

**Missing:** `<meta name="description">`, `<link rel="canonical">`, og:title, og:description, og:image, twitter:card.  
**Title tag** is "Northview Psychology — AI Receptionist" (client branding, not TRIVOX).

**Impact:** If indexed, the page ranks for a competitor/demo clinic brand and has no TRIVOX meta. Not a critical issue for users, but it's a mild SEO and brand leak.

**Fix options:** Either add a `noindex` meta + remove from sitemap (preferred for demo pages), or add TRIVOX-branded SEO meta and remove the client name from the title.
