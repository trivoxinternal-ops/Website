## 4. Hidden-Content / Mobile Bugs

**Result: CLEAN**

- `ds/motion.css` uses `[data-reveal]` → `opacity:0` pattern. `motion.js` activates via IntersectionObserver with **`threshold: 0.15`** (safe — well below ≥0.4 risk zone) and `rootMargin: '0px 0px -8% 0px'` ✅
- `prefers-reduced-motion` forces `opacity:1 !important` and `transform:none !important` on all reveals ✅
- `ds/scroll.js` (GSAP ScrollTrigger) animates **transform only, not opacity** — explicitly noted in source comments so no element can get stuck invisible ✅
- No high-threshold reveals that could cause black gaps on mobile.
