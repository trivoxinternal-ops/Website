- **Threshold: 0.15** (15%) — safe for mobile. Only 15% of the element needs to enter the viewport to trigger the reveal. No risk of tall elements staying permanently hidden.

- Fallback: if IntersectionObserver is unavailable, all reveals are immediately shown via `el.classList.add('in')`. ✅
