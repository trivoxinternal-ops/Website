## 6. Hidden-Content / Mobile Reveal Pattern — Clean ✅

The `[data-reveal]` pattern in `ds/motion.css` starts elements at `opacity:0; transform:translateY(26px)` and transitions to visible on scroll.

`ds/motion.js` uses `IntersectionObserver` with **threshold: 0.15** and `rootMargin: '0px 0px -8% 0px'`. This is a safe threshold — well below the 0.4 risk zone where tall elements on mobile viewports can fail to intersect and stay permanently hidden.

GSAP `ScrollTrigger` reveals in index.html use `start: 'top 82%–88%'` which also gives generous early-trigger margins. No stuck-hidden risk identified.

`prefers-reduced-motion` is respected: reveals immediately add `.in` class, GSAP scroll animations bail early.
