## 5. Hidden Content / Mobile Bugs (Reveal Pattern)

**✅ Safe — no stuck-element risk found.**

- `motion.css` hides `[data-reveal]` elements at `opacity:0; transform:translateY(26px)`.
- `motion.js` uses `IntersectionObserver` with `threshold: 0.15` (15%) and `rootMargin: '0px 0px -8% 0px'`. A 15% threshold is low and safe — even a tall section on mobile will easily cross it.
- The inline GSAP comment in `index.html` confirms the design intent: **GSAP animates transform only, motion.js handles opacity.** Worst case if GSAP misfires: elements slide in without animation but are never invisible.
- The `.vault.reveal` section uses class-based reveal (not `[data-reveal]`), set by GSAP at `start:'top 70%'` — also safe, GSAP only touches transform/position.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected — motion.css immediately makes all `[data-reveal]` elements visible when reduce motion is on.
