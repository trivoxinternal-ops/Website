## 4. Hidden-Content / Mobile Bugs

**✅ Clean.**

- `ds/motion.css` uses `[data-reveal]` with `opacity:0` and `transform:translateY(26px)` as the hidden state.
- `ds/motion.js` reveals elements via `IntersectionObserver` with **threshold: 0.15** and `rootMargin: '0px 0px -8% 0px'`.
- Threshold of 0.15 is well within the safe range — only 15% of the element needs to enter the viewport to trigger. No risk of elements staying permanently hidden on mobile due to high threshold.
- `motion.css` includes a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` block that forces all `[data-reveal]` elements immediately visible (`opacity:1 !important; transform:none !important`). This is a good safety net.
- No `display:none` or `visibility:hidden` content patterns detected that would cause permanent invisible sections.
