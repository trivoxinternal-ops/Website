AI automation in Mississauga, answered.

What does an AI automation agency in Mississauga do?

We design and build custom systems that run your operations automatically: business process automation, custom software, data and reporting engines, CRM systems, and AI voice and chat agents. They're complete, done-for-you systems, so you never have to stitch together disconnected AI tools yourself.

How much does AI process automation cost?

Most projects combine a one-time setup fee to design and deploy the system with an optional monthly management fee. The final number depends on scope, which we make clear before you commit. You get a tailored quote on a free intro call.

Do you serve businesses outside Mississauga?

Yes. We serve Mississauga and all of Peel Region and the Greater Toronto Area in person, plus businesses across the rest of Canada remotely.

Which Mississauga businesses benefit most?

Lead-driven and operations-heavy businesses: real estate, dealerships along Dundas and the QEW, healthcare and dental clinics, home service businesses, logistics and warehousing near Pearson, and professional services.

What's the difference between an AI process automation company and AI workflow automation?

AI process automation is the whole job of removing manual steps from a business process. AI workflow automation is how it gets wired: connecting your CRM, email and finance apps so data moves and tasks fire on their own. TRIVOX builds the workflow automation underneath, on n8n, Make, Airtable and GoHighLevel.
