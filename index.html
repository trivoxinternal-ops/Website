Answered

The questions everyone asks.

What is AI lead generation?

AI lead generation uses AI to bring you more customers: making AI assistants and search engines recommend you (GEO), running and optimizing Meta, Google and TikTok ads, building websites that convert, and following up with leads automatically. We build it as a done-for-you system for Toronto businesses.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT or Google's AI?

Your customers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini for recommendations instead of scrolling Google. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) structures your site, content and data so those models cite and recommend you. We run a free AI visibility check, then optimize so the AI names you, not a competitor. Read the full guide: how to get recommended by ChatGPT.

Do you only build chatbots and voice agents?

No. Conversational agents are just the entry layer. Most of our work is custom AI software and systems: automation platforms, marketplaces, reporting and finance engines, CRM and database automation, built around how your business runs.

How long until something is live?

Most first systems go live in about nine days, measured from kickoff to a working system in production. Larger platforms are scoped with a clear timeline.

Do I have to manage any code?

No. We build it, host it and maintain it. You approve the flows, the system runs.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Every critical action passes through your approval before it goes out. You never lose control.

Which tools do you use?

VAPI, n8n, Make, Airtable and GoHighLevel, plus custom code when a system needs it. Assembled around your existing stack, not a rip-and-replace.
