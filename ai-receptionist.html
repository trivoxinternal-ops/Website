What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is software that answers your business phone the way a front-desk person would: it greets the caller, answers common questions, qualifies them, books appointments into your calendar and takes messages, 24 hours a day. We build them for Toronto and GTA businesses, tuned to your scripts, hours and calendar, so no call goes unanswered.

How is it different from a normal answering service?

A traditional answering service takes a message and passes it on later. An automated AI receptionist handles the call: it answers the question, checks live availability and books the appointment in the same conversation, then logs it to your CRM. No callback gap, no per-operator staffing cost.

Does it sound natural or robotic?

It uses natural speech with realistic pacing and interruption handling, so most callers have a normal conversation. We tune the voice, tone and script to match your brand and test it before it ever answers a live customer.

Can it book appointments and text back missed calls?

Yes. It checks live availability and books directly into your calendar, and if a call is ever missed it can send an automatic text back so the lead does not go cold. Every call and outcome is logged to your CRM.

What does an AI receptionist cost in Toronto?

It combines a one-time build fee to design, script and deploy it with an ongoing fee for call minutes and management. Cost depends on call volume and how much you want it to handle. You get a tailored quote on a free intro call.
