What is an AI voice agent?

An AI voice agent is a human-like AI receptionist that answers calls 24/7, handles inquiries, qualifies callers, and books appointments or routes them to the right person. We build them on VAPI, tuned to your business, scripts and calendar.

Is an AI answering service better than voicemail?

Voicemail loses callers; most people hang up and call a competitor. An AI answering service picks up on the first ring, has a real conversation, answers questions, and books the appointment while intent is high, so no call goes to waste.

Does the AI receptionist sound robotic?

No. It uses natural speech with realistic pacing and interruption handling, so callers have a normal conversation. We tune the voice, tone and script to match your brand before it goes live.

Can it book appointments and route calls?

Yes. It qualifies the caller, checks live availability, books directly into your calendar, and can transfer or escalate complex calls to a human, logging every call and outcome to your CRM.

What does an AI voice agent cost?

It combines a one-time build fee to design, script and deploy the agent with an ongoing fee for call minutes and management. Cost depends on volume and complexity. You get a tailored quote on a free intro call.
