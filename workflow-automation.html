Answered

Workflow automation, answered.

What is workflow automation?

Workflow automation connects your tools so a trigger in one app fires actions in the others without manual work. We build custom automations that move data between CRM, finance, email and apps, run follow-ups, and generate reports, so repetitive processes run themselves.

How is it different from business process automation?

Workflow automation handles individual task chains; business process automation strings those chains into whole end-to-end processes, like lead-to-close or quote-to-invoice. We design both, starting with your highest-friction process and expanding from there.

Which tools do you use?

We build on n8n, Make, Airtable and GoHighLevel, plus custom code when a process needs it. We assemble around your existing stack rather than forcing a rip-and-replace, so your team keeps the tools it already uses.

Can you automate CRM follow-ups and reporting?

Yes. We automate CRM data entry, pipeline updates, follow-up sequences, internal notifications, and recurring reports, so leads never go cold and reports are ready without anyone building them by hand.

How much does it cost?

Projects combine a one-time build fee with an optional monthly management fee. Cost depends on the number and complexity of processes. You get a tailored quote on a free intro call.
